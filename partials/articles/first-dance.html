What do you both love listening to?

That's the first thing I'd ask you. Before we get into wedding playlists, I'd want to know what you enjoy listening to together.

Maybe there's a song from a trip, an artist you both love, or something that always ends up playing in the car. I'd put those on the shortlist. You don't need a big story behind the song if you both love hearing it.

If nothing comes to mind, my suggestion is to pick a few songs each and listen together. And I wouldn't rule out a favourite just because you've heard it at someone else's wedding.

Try dancing to it at home

Before you settle on anything, I'd put it on at home and have a dance together. Nothing rehearsed. Just see whether you can find the beat and a pace you're comfortable with.

A song can sound beautiful through headphones and feel surprisingly tricky to move to. If you're thinking so hard about your feet that you've stopped enjoying the music, I'd try another from your list.

Taking lessons? Bring your shortlist to your teacher too. They'll be able to help with the dance itself. Let them know what you'll be wearing and how much room you'll have on the floor.

Send me the version you love

One detail I'd check before you commit: the rest of the lyrics. It's easy to know a chorus by heart without having paid much attention to the verses. Have a listen all the way through and see how you feel about it.

You might prefer an acoustic cover or an instrumental version. That's worth trying, but check it by dancing to it too. The pace and introduction can be quite different from the original.

Once you've chosen, send me the exact link so we're listening to the same recording. That's the one to practise with, especially if you're learning a routine.

How long do you want the floor to yourselves?

I'd talk this through with you as well as the song choice. If you love the idea of dancing to the whole thing, great. If you'd feel more comfortable with a shorter section, let's talk about where it could finish naturally.

You can also invite your guests in partway through. Let me know if that's what you'd like, and we can agree when the invitation happens and who's making it. It helps to have that settled before the music starts.

And if one of you is nervous about being alone on the floor, tell me. I'd rather we talk about that while we're planning. You don't have to put on a big performance to have a first dance you enjoy.

Before you decide

  • Do we both enjoy this song?
  • Have we tried moving to it together?
  • Have we picked the exact recording?
  • Have we agreed how the dance will end?

Bring whatever you've got to our music chat, even if it's just a couple of songs you're unsure about. You don't need it all worked out before you get in touch.
