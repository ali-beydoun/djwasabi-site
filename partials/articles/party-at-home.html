Decide when the music comes down

Pick a finish time before you pick any songs, and plan the last half hour as a wind-down. NSW Police's party advice says much the same: give the party a definite start and finish, and turn the music down as you get near the end.

Tell your neighbours the plan and give them a number they can call you on. Let me know the time too, so the music can settle well before then.

Know the hours for music at home

In NSW, the Environment Protection Authority publishes restricted hours for music and amplified sound from a home. During those hours it shouldn't be heard inside a residential room in a neighbour's home, such as a bedroom or living room. The quiet hours start at midnight on Friday and Saturday nights and on the night before a public holiday. On other nights they start at 10pm. In each case they run until 8am the next morning. It's an audibility rule for homes, not a switch-off time for all music. Check the EPA's neighbourhood noise page or your council for the current detail.

Outside those restricted hours, council or police can still act on offensive noise and direct you to turn it down or stop. Letting your neighbours know in advance is a courtesy that sits alongside those rules; it doesn't replace them.

Tell me where people will be

Send me a couple of photos and tell me where your guests will gather, where the nearest power points are, and which side the closest neighbours are on. We can talk through where the speakers could sit before the day. Nobody wants to discover on the night that the only outlet is in the kitchen.

I'd keep the party in the backyard or an enclosed area rather than out the front, which is also what the police advise. It's easier to see who's arriving, and it's usually further from the street. A garage or an undercover area changes both the sound and the weather plan, so tell me if that's where the dancing will be.

Have a plan for the weather

Decide now which part of the party would move under cover if it rains, and where equipment could go. Tell me whether the music will be inside, under cover or in the open, so we can talk about what works in your space.
