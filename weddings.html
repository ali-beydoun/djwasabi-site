Common Questions

How far in advance should I book?

For peak wedding season (September-May), we recommend booking 6-12 months in advance. Popular Saturday dates fill quickly!

How do we choose our first dance song?

I'd start with what you both love listening to, then try dancing to a few favourites at home. Once you've chosen, send me the exact version. We can talk through how long you'd like the floor to yourselves and whether you'd like your guests to join you. Read my guide to choosing your first dance song.

Do you take song requests from guests?

I'm happy to take requests that fit your celebration's vibe. You can also provide a do-not-play list.

Can you provide ceremony music?

I can cover your ceremony with processional, signing, and recessional music using a separate wireless setup.

Do you have backup equipment?

Always. I bring backup speakers, microphones, and music players to every event.

Can we include music from different cultures?

Yes. I'd start with the traditions and songs that matter to each of you. Send me the exact recordings and tell me about any family dances or separately booked performers, so we can discuss how they fit into the reception. Read my advice on music for a multicultural wedding.

Should we hire an MC or ask a friend?

A willing friend can bring a personal touch, while a professional takes on the hosting so your guests can enjoy the night. I'd weigh the responsibilities, the cost and who you'd both feel comfortable with. Read my thoughts on choosing your wedding MC.

What's included in MC services?

Announcements, introductions, vendor coordination, and running the schedule so you don't have to.
