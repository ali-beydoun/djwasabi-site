Common Questions

Can you work with our event coordinator?

I regularly work with event planners, coordinators, and venue staff. I'll fit into your event program, not the other way around.

Are you insured for corporate events?

Yes, I maintain appropriate insurance coverage for corporate events and can discuss specific requirements with your team.

Can you handle presentations?

Yes! I can manage audio for presentations, videos, and speeches with professional wireless microphones and audio mixing.

What about dress code?

I always dress appropriately for corporate events - typically business formal unless otherwise specified.

Can you provide branded content?

Yes, I can incorporate your company jingles, theme songs, or branded audio content into the event.

Do you offer rates for multiple events?

Yes, if your company has several events throughout the year, let's chat about ongoing arrangements.
